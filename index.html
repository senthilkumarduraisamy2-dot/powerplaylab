MASTER DIGGER

Take the controls.
Become the operator.

Ever wondered what it feels like to operate a JCB? Sit in the operator's seat, take control with both hands and experience the thrill of digging, lifting, moving and dropping.

It's not a video game. You're actually controlling the machine.

🏗️ Operate the excavator🪣 Dig and scoop↔️ Move and position🎯 Improve control & precision
The challenge: Can you dig like an operator?
